Chalk & Bar

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Chalk & Bar does not collect any personal data. Your training data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored by the app

The app stores the information you enter – training blocks, workouts, logged sets, exercises, maxes, notes, bodyweight and settings (such as units, plates, rest times and the optional age group and training experience used by the program finder) – on your iPhone.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is on (Settings → iCloud), this data is also stored in your private iCloud database and iCloud key-value storage, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This storage belongs to your Apple account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off at any time; the data then stays only on your iPhone.

Apple Health

With your permission, the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate and active energy during a workout and saves the finished workout (type, duration, heart rate, calories) to Apple Health. Health data is used only to show these values during the workout and to save the workout to Health. It is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with the developer or third parties. You can change or remove access in the Health app → Sharing → Apps.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and the Apple Watch exchange the open workout and logged sets directly over Apple's Watch Connectivity. Nothing is sent to any server.

Export

When you export your training log (Settings → Data), a CSV file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

What the developer receives

Nothing from inside the app. The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs and makes no network requests to the developer. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports. If you e-mail support, your message and e-mail address are used only to answer you.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected by the developer, there is no data held about you to access, correct or delete. Data in the app can be deleted inside the app, by deleting the app, and in your iCloud settings (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
