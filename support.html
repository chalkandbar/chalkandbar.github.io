Frequently asked questions

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your blocks, workouts and settings sync through your private iCloud account. Sign in with the same Apple ID on the new iPhone, keep Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud on, and your data appears after a moment. You can also export your training log as a CSV file in Settings → Data.

The Apple Watch app doesn't show my workout.

Open the workout on your iPhone first – the Watch shows the workout that is open on the iPhone. Make sure the Chalk & Bar Watch app is installed (Watch app on iPhone → Available Apps) and Bluetooth is on.

Heart rate and calories aren't recorded.

Allow Health access when the Watch asks, or later in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Chalk & Bar.

How are the suggested weights calculated?

From your estimated 1RM (e1RM): today's logged sets first, then your recent best sets, then the max you entered. Variations without their own history are estimated from your squat, bench or deadlift. Adjust with RPE – the next sets follow how the weight actually felt.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
